FAQ

Frequently asked questions

PRODUCT

When should I take it, before or after drinking?

We recommend taking it before drinking.

I find pills hard to swallow. Can I crush them?

Yes, you can crush them before taking.

How many sticks can I take a day?

Hwakggaeu is formulated as a food, so there is no set limit. We do not recommend taking too much, however.

Can I take it with a sports drink?

Yes, that is fine.

Can teenagers take it?

It is a food made from herbal ingredients, so teenagers can take it.

Can I take it on days I am not drinking?

Yes, you can take it any day.

Is it safe during pregnancy or while taking medication?

If you are pregnant or breastfeeding, have a medical condition or are taking medication, please consult a professional before taking it.

How should I store it?

Store it at room temperature. A place away from direct sunlight is best.

Does it contain allergens?

Hwakggaeu itself contains no allergenic ingredients. It is, however, made in a facility that also processes eggs (poultry), milk, soybeans, wheat, buckwheat, peanuts, crab, shrimp, pork, peach, sulfites, tomato, walnuts, chicken, beef, squid, pine nuts and shellfish (including oysters, abalone and mussels). Please keep this in mind if you are allergic to any of these.

INGREDIENTS & PRODUCTION

Are the ingredients from Korea?

Most are from Korea. The Atractylodes lancea powder is from China.

What is GMP certification?

GMP stands for Good Manufacturing Practice. In Korea, the Ministry of Food and Drug Safety reviews hygiene and quality control across the whole process, from raw-material intake to manufacturing, packaging and shipping, before granting it. Hwakggaeu is produced at HACCP and GMP certified sites.

Why is it priced lower than similar products?

We optimised our production process, keep distribution margins low by selling directly, and use ingredients with a stable supply.
